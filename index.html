The bigger picture

Charging is more than plugging in.

The move towards electric vehicles brings new questions about where charging happens, how it fits into everyday routines and what different environments may require.

A home driveway, a workplace car park and a vehicle fleet have different considerations. Understanding the context is an important first step before choosing a charging direction.

Start with the setting. Understand the requirement. Then explore the right charging direction.

A considered approach

Clarity before complexity.

The right charging decision starts with understanding the situation rather than assuming every requirement is the same.

01

Understand the environment

Identify where vehicles are parked, how they are used and what the charging setting needs to accommodate.

02

Define the requirement

Consider usage patterns, available space, electrical capacity and the expectations of the people using the site.

03

Explore suitable options

Compare relevant charging approaches and technical requirements before deciding what may be appropriate.

04

Plan the next step

Establish what information, technical review and professional input would be needed to move forward.
